Fish Riddle Club is an illustrated ocean quiz. Study a fish, listen to the clue, and choose its name. Each correct answer unlocks a short fact and adds the fish to your field guide.

• Solve 16 picture riddles across four expeditions.
• Meet fish from coral reefs, the open ocean, and the deep sea.
• Hear every clue and fish fact in English. Tap to replay the narration.
• Revisit your discoveries in the field guide and replay any expedition.
• Play offline with optional music. Your progress is saved on your device.
