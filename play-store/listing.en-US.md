Use the two description text files for the listing. Upload the PNG icon and feature graphic from this folder. Upload the four images in each device folder in filename order. The phone images frame actual gameplay in a device outline. The tablet and desktop sets show the large-screen app interface.

The MP4 is prepared for a separate YouTube upload. Set visibility to Public or Unlisted, disable monetization, and leave the video free of age restrictions. Use the resulting YouTube URL in Play Console.

Gameplay runs offline. App progress and settings remain in private device storage. The game has no ads, analytics, accounts, runtime permissions, or third-party network SDKs. Google Play handles purchase and distribution. Set your paid price and distribution countries in Play Console.
