FISH RIDDLE CLUB

Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026

PT Deploy Dulu Pulang Nanti publishes Fish Riddle Club for Android. This policy describes how the game handles information and what happens if you contact support.

Data in the game

The game does not collect or transmit personal information, create accounts, or use advertising or analytics services. The riddles, illustrations, English narration, and music are included in the installation. The game plays them locally and does not use the microphone.

Progress and preferences

The fish you discover and your audio settings are stored in the game's private storage on your device. We do not receive that information. You can remove it by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings or uninstalling the app.

Google Play and external links

Google Play handles the purchase and installation. We do not receive your payment details through the game. When you open this policy from the game, it opens in your browser. Google Play and the website host handle their services under their own privacy policies.

Contacting support

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information to respond and resolve the issue. We retain support correspondence as needed for that purpose and to meet legal obligations. You can contact us to request deletion of a support message, subject to applicable obligations.
